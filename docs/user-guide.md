# Staff guide — getting started

For motel staff. Share this page, or just send the install link below.

## The links

| What | Link |
|---|---|
| Portal (sign in) | https://dm-ops-production.vercel.app |
| Install instructions | https://dm-ops-production.vercel.app/install |

Your manager creates your account and gives you your email and a temporary password.

## Install the app on your phone

The portal can sit on your home screen as **DMO**, with its own icon (the white
bed on blue), and open full-screen like an app. There's nothing to download from an app store.

### Android (Chrome)

1. Open the portal link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu (top right).
3. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**), then **Install**.

Chrome may also show an **Install** button on the login page or on the Services
page, which does the same in one tap.

### iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1. Open the portal link in **Safari**. Chrome on iPhone may not offer this.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow) at the bottom.
3. Scroll down, tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

### Good to know

- You still sign in with your email and password. You stay signed in for 7 days.
- It needs an internet connection.
- **Old icon or name?** Delete the shortcut, reload the portal in the browser, and add it again.
- The app version is shown at the bottom of every page (e.g. `v4.7.6`).
- On a phone, the Services page shows an "Add to home screen" card until the app
  is installed. Tap **✕** to hide it on that phone.

## Daily Cleanliness Inspection

- Tap the **Daily Cleanliness Inspection** card to see the list of days.
  **Today** is at the top — tap **Start today's inspection** (or **Continue**).
- At the top of the sheet you always see **which day** it is, with a green
  **Today** or amber **Past date** label. Use **◀ / ▶** or the date box to change day.
- **Past days are locked.** A missed day shows as a blank sheet. If a past day
  needs filling in or fixing, ask a manager to **Unlock** it; you can then edit it
  until the manager locks it again or marks it complete.
- **Managers:** the **Performance** tab on the day list shows how each room is
  doing over the last 7, 30 or 90 days — a score per room (green / amber / red),
  its trend, the checklist items that fail most, and a problem map of all rooms.
  Tap a room for its day-by-day record; tap a day to open that sheet.

## Notifications

- The **bell** at the top shows new updates — e.g. a room assigned to you, a room
  ready for you to inspect, a room sent back with the reason. Tap one to go
  straight to it.
- **Get alerts on your phone:** open the bell → **See all · Notification settings**
  → **Turn on** under *Phone notifications on this device*, and allow it when the
  phone asks. On iPhone, add DMO to your home screen first and open it from there.
  Do this once on each phone or computer you use.
- On the same page you can switch off the types you don't want.

## Messages

- Tap the **speech-bubble** icon at the top for **Messages**.
- **New message** → pick a person for a private chat. Only the two of you can read it.
- **Groups** are automatic: **All staff**, plus one for your role (e.g. Housekeepers).
  Tap **Mute** in a group to stop its notifications.
- **Announcements** from managers appear at the top. You can read them, not reply;
  the manager can see who has seen it.
- **Comments:** open a housekeeping task (or, for managers, a room's history) and
  use **Comments** to leave notes for the team about that room or task.

## Signing in

- Use the email your manager set up. Five wrong passwords in 15 minutes locks the
  account for a few minutes.
- Forgot your password? Ask a manager to reset it.
